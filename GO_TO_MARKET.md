# HealthPulse - go to market

**Live app:** (to be added)  |  **Code:** https://github.com/Choquri2000/healthpulse

## One-liner
Upload your Apple Health export and see, in plain English, what changed in your own numbers - without sending your data anywhere.

## Landing copy
> **Your Apple Health data, finally readable.**
> Your iPhone has years of steps, sleep and heart-rate data, buried in a huge file. Drop it in and get:
> "Your resting heart rate is 4 bpm above your longer-term average." "You're going to bed 38 minutes later than last month."
> Private by design: processed in memory, never stored, no AI services. Free.
> *Descriptive trends only - not medical advice.*

## Who it helps
- Apple Watch / iPhone owners curious about their long-term trends, who find the Health app's charts shallow.
- Quantified-self hobbyists, runners and people tracking weight or sleep routines.
- Privacy-minded users who do not want to upload health data to an account-based service.

## Honest validation plan
1. Test with 5-10 real exports from friends/volunteers (different iOS versions, Watch vs iPhone-only, large files). Not yet tested on a real export - only synthetic data.
2. Post in relevant communities where self-promotion is permitted (r/AppleWatch, r/QuantifiedSelf rules permitting - read rules first), ask for feedback not signups.
3. Measure: uploads completed vs. abandoned, "which finding was useful" feedback. Success = people say they learned something and come back with a new export.
4. Check whether the 200 MB cloud limit blocks many users; if so, offer a tiny local helper script.

## Free monetisation ideas (no guarantees)
- Optional "buy me a coffee" / tip link.
- Free tool as a top-of-funnel for a paid local desktop version (no size limits, PDF report).
- Affiliate links are risky in a health context - avoid unless clearly separate and honest.

## Limits / caveats
Not a medical device; no advice; thresholds are simple heuristics; sleep nights are inferred from sleep segments; no guarantee of users, revenue or accuracy.
